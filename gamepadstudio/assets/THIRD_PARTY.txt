GamePadStudio macOS preview dependencies

The macOS bundle includes FFmpeg 7.1 from the architecture-matched
imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 wheel. FFmpeg is licensed under GPL version 2 or later
in this build (built with --enable-gpl). Run bin/ffmpeg -L and
bin/ffmpeg -buildconf for the binary's license and build configuration.

FFmpeg project and source:
https://ffmpeg.org/
https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz
Wheel packaging and binary build project:
https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg
https://github.com/imageio/imageio-binaries

Other dependencies retain their licenses and notices supplied with them.
